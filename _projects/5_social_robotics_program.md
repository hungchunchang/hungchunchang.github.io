## English

The Social Robotics Program is a series of workshops and training programs designed for undergraduate students in the domain of social robotics. The program covers hands-on topics including:

- **Multimodal data analysis** (speech, gesture, physiological signals)
- **Voice-based social robot** design and implementation
- Literature review and user testing methodology
- Prototyping and basic programming for social robots

To date, the program has organized **2 workshops** and **2 training programs**, with sessions totaling **18+ hours** for 11 students.

## 中文

社交機器人培訓課程是一套針對大學生設計的工作坊與培訓計畫，涵蓋社交機器人領域的實作主題，包括：

- **多模態資料分析**（語音、手勢、生理訊號等）
- **語音型社交機器人**的設計與實作
- 文獻回顧方法與使用者測試技巧
- 社交機器人的原型製作與基礎程式設計

目前已舉辦 **2 場工作坊** 與 **2 個培訓課程**，共為 11 名學生提供逾 **18 小時**的教學內容。
