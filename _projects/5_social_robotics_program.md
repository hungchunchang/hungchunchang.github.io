**[GitHub](https://github.com/hungchunchang/SocialRoboticsProgram)**

The Social Robotics Training Program is a series of workshops and training programs designed for undergraduate students in the domain of social robotics. The program covers hands-on topics including:

- **Multimodal data analysis** (speech, gesture, physiological signals)
- **Voice-based social robot** design and implementation
- Literature review and user testing methodology
- Prototyping and basic programming for social robots

To date, the program has organized **2 workshops** and **2 training programs**, with sessions totaling **18+ hours** for 11 students.
