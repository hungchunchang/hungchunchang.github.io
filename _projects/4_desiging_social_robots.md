**Initiator & sole developer** · Feb. 2025 – present · now in beta

The Social Robot Design Platform distills the workflow behind the social robots I built for the lab into a design language, and implements it as a web-based end-user programming tool so that researchers can build robot behavior without writing code.

- **Translated** social robotics system development workflows into a Social Robot Design Language, inspired by my experience of developing 5 social robotic systems.
- **Implemented** the design language as a web-based end-user programming tool.
- **Collaborated** with four groups of student researchers in a research-through-design process over 6 months.
- **Deployed** the system in a laboratory experiment for over a month.

## The systems behind it

The design language grew out of systems I developed and deployed with real users:

- **XiaoXiao**, a [companion robot for older adults](/projects/3_robots_for_elderly/)
- A **diary robot**
- **Museum guide robots** — for the centennial exhibition at the Iso Eikichi Cottage, where visitors talked with the robot after their tour {% cite lin2025museum %}, and for the opening of the NTU College of Engineering museum
- An **opening-ceremony host robot** at the National Taiwan Library
- **[ECRobot](/projects/1_ecrobot/)**, a robot tutor

This development work also became the basis of the lab's [Social Robotics Training Program](/projects/5_social_robotics_program/).
